🌌 Final Scene — Our Future
Niku ❤️ Helly

MY LETTER TO YOU

Helly,

I don't know how to begin a letter like this, because the feelings I have for you are bigger than any words I know.

When I was small, I used to wonder if love was real — the kind that stays, the kind that's patient, the kind that chooses you every single day even when it's hard. And then I found you.

You are the reason I believe in that kind of love now. The way you laugh, the way you get angry over small things (and somehow look cute doing it), the way you say my name — it all means the world to me.

I want to promise you this:

💕 I will never cheat. Never.
💕 I will always be loyal — to you and only you.
💕 I will always be honest, even when it's hard.
💕 I will try every day to understand your feelings, your moods, your silences.

I don't know what the future holds, but I know I want to face it with you by my side. Every lake, every chaat, every long call, every hug — I want a million more of them with you.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for being mine.

Forever yours,
Niku ❤️
